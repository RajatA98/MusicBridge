# Expert Candidates — Piano Home Practice BrainLift

Verified via WebSearch May 2026. Mix of academic researchers and practitioners.

## Academic Researchers

| Name | Affiliation | Specific contribution / claim | Camp | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Robert A. Duke | UT Austin (Center for Music Learning) | "It's Not How Much; It's How" (2009) — eight specific error-handling behaviors distinguish top advanced pianists from the rest. Direct evidence for the HOW belief. | Strategy / behavioral | Journal of Research in Music Education 2009 |
| Susan Hallam | UCL Institute of Education (Emeritus) | Practice strategy taxonomy across expertise levels — novices use whole-piece run-throughs without error correction regardless of time invested. Direct evidence for the strategy gap. | Strategy / expertise | Music Education Research, Psychology of Music |
| Peter Miksza | Indiana University Jacobs School of Music | Specific written practice goals reviewed at next lesson improve practice quality at d ≈ 0.4–0.6 vs. generic "practice this piece." Direct evidence for the WHAT belief. | Self-regulation | Journal of Research in Music Education 2011, 2015 |
| Gary E. McPherson | University of Melbourne (Melbourne Conservatorium) | Longitudinal beginner studies; pre-learning *commitment* predicts achievement better than weekly practice minutes; failure-spiral framing connecting practice quality to dropout. | Self-regulation / metacognition | Psychology of Music 2005; with Evans 2013 |
| Akiho Suzuki, Jane Ginsborg, Michelle Phillips, Zoe Franklin | Royal Northern College of Music | 2024: developed an online intervention to equip tertiary piano students with explicit skills and strategies for effective practice. Direct evidence that the skill of practicing is teachable. | Strategy / intervention design | Sage Journals, Music Performance 2024 |
| Leon R. de Bruin | University of Melbourne / RMIT | 2023, 2024: feedback in the instrumental lesson is the "missing link" — frames teacher feedback as the breakdown point between lesson and home practice. | Feedback / translation | Music Education Research 2023, 2024 |
| Brooke N. Macnamara | Case Western Reserve University | 2014 meta-analysis: deliberate practice explains only ~21% of variance in music performance. Kills the "more time" framing of the motivation-and-quantity industry. | Anti-deliberate-practice / individual differences | Psychological Science 2014 |
| Edward L. Deci & Richard M. Ryan | University of Rochester | Self-Determination Theory: tangible rewards undermine intrinsic motivation; controlling environments backfire. The motivation counter-argument honored. | Motivation / SDT | Meta-analyses 1999, 2017 |
| Jennifer Blackwell | with McPherson | 2025: teacher feedback in collegiate instrumental music lessons. Extends McPherson's lineage into feedback specifics. | Self-regulation / feedback | Sage Journals 2025 |
